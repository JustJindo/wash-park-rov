I love ROVs, so I'm making one of my own. I initially made an ROV to dive to the bottom of Smith Lake in Denver, CO's Washington Park. Now, I'm redesigning my ROV to explore the waters of Northern California. I created this repository to document the increasingly cursed solutions I use to make electronics swim.
If you share an interest in making ROVs, I hope this repository's contents can help you avoid some pitfalls and enhance your own designs.

UPDATE 06/13/2026: I tested the first working prototype of my ROV in Smith Lake today! My ROV made it to the bottom of the lake, albeit upside down. There are a number of weight distribution issues to address, and the endcap flange seals have proven themselves to be too tight. Fortunately, all the electronics managed to survive my ROV's first dive, and I have A LOT of new considerations for my next prototype.
